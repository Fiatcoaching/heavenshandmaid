Meet the Maker

Hi, I’m Cassandra.

Heavens Handmaid started in 2017 out of my home in Chico, California, with a hook, a skein of yarn, and a desire to make things that carry a little bit of prayer into everyday life. Every piece is made to order by hand — no assembly line, no shortcuts, just stitches worked one at a time.

What began as cozy beanies and market-stall accessories has grown into devotional keepsakes named for the saints who inspire our family — nursery pillows, rattles, and loveys meant to wrap little ones in faith from their very first days.

— Cassandra
